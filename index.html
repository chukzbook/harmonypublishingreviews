Yes — it is the live rating on our verified Google Business Profile for Harmony Publishing at 2201 Menaul Blvd NE Ste A, Albuquerque, NM 87107. The link above opens that profile directly.

Where do reviews actually live?

Every review is hosted on the platform where the author left it — Google Business Profile, Trustpilot or StarOfService. This page links to each profile rather than copying text, so you can always verify a review at its source.

Yes — we serve authors in both markets, and feedback from any published author is welcome on any of the channels above.
